# Code of Conduct

We want participation in AJ's Character Manager to be respectful, useful, and
welcoming.

Contributors are expected to communicate constructively, respect different
levels of experience and points of view, accept well-reasoned feedback, and
focus criticism on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments that violate
these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when necessary. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately, and made in the project's best interest.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through their
GitHub contact information. Do not disclose sensitive details in a public
issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
